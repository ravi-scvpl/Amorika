Little Intentions, waiting to unfold

Basket | Warm. Woven. Rooted +

Handcrafted from natural kauna grass in Manipur. A beautiful, time-honoured craft bringing warmth and character into any space.


A tactile piece with a life beyond the hamper. A place for memorabilia, letters, keepsakes, picnic essentials or everyday treasures. A beautiful reminder that even the simplest things can be worth keeping close.

Singing Bowl | Resonant. Grounding. Timeless +

Shaped through repeated hammering, its deep resonance creates a moment of pause. A gentle invitation towards stillness, grounding and coming back to the present.


A sound that fades slowly. A little more quiet left behind in the moment.

Flower Tea With Glass Infuser | Gentle. Floral. Soothing +

Carefully picked chamomile, blue corn & rose buds flower tea, paired with a glass infuser for moments when slowing down and breathing a little easier feels just right.


Flowers unfolding in warm water. A gentle floral aroma. A slow sip. A softer moment around the day.

Wax Sachets | Aromatic. Comforting. Atmospheric +

Hand-poured fragrant soy wax sachets created to gently change the feeling of spaces around the home.

Rainforest: Fresh, earthy and calming.

Coffee: Warm, rich and familiar.

A little aromatic comfort for linens, wardrobes, drawers, restrooms or even cars. A softer atmosphere wherever a little lift is needed.

Linen Cotton Napkins | Natural. Textured. Everyday +

A beautiful & tactile cotton-linen weave, chosen to bring a little more warmth and ease to the everyday table.


For morning tea, a slow meal, coffee with someone worth talking to. Small everyday gatherings, made a little more special through texture, warmth and shared presence.
